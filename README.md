## Objective
What is the key pain point?

As an MCA graduate and aspiring Data Analyst, I want to understand how businesses use data analytics to evaluate sales performance, identify trends, and support strategic decision-making.

Analyzing large sales datasets manually can make it difficult to identify top-performing products, profitable regions, effective sales channels, and overall business performance.

## User Story
As an MCA graduate and aspiring Data Analyst, I want to analyze Adidas US sales data using Power BI to identify business trends, measure performance, and uncover insights that support data-driven decisions.

The dashboard should allow me to explore sales across products, regions, retailers, and sales methods using interactive visualizations and key performance indicators.

Through this project, I aim to strengthen my practical skills in data cleaning, data transformation, DAX calculations, business intelligence, and analytical problem-solving

## Data Source
Dataset: Adidas US Sales Dataset

  *The dataset contains information about:
  
  *Retailers and products
  
  *Invoice dates
  
  *Regions, states, and cities
  
  *Price per unit
  
  *Units sold
  
  *Total sales
  
  *Operating profit and margin
  
  *Sales methods
